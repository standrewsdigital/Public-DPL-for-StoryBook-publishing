What is masters study?

In a taught postgraduate or masters programme, you would typically have lectures, seminars and assessments. Depending on the length of your studies, you could achieve an MLitt, MSc, MRes, PGDip or PGCert.

What is postgraduate research?

Postgraduate research focuses on an independent research project where you work closely with academic supervisors to create a substantial piece of work which is examined at the end of the programme.
